# Prohibited and restricted jobs - pilot draft

## Prohibited
Illegal acts/goods, violence/weapons, sexual exploitation, trafficking, child labor, unauthorized entry/surveillance, asbestos/explosives, live high-voltage, structural demolition, confined spaces, hazardous chemicals, medical procedures, gas work, and any licensed work whose authorization cannot be verified.

## Restricted or not yet open
Late-night/isolated work, occupied-home domestic/care work, roof/ladder work, construction sites, transport of valuables, high-value access, driving/security, and other categories requiring controls the pilot has not built.

## User message behavior
Do not debate or help reframe a prohibited request. Explain the category is unavailable, preserve/report only what policy/law requires, and offer a safe lawful alternative where appropriate. Imminent danger routes to the safety flow and reviewed emergency guidance.
