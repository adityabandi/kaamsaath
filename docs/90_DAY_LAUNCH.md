# Jothe 30/60/90-day cell launch

All budgets and thresholds are planning assumptions. Nothing here authorizes spend, staffing, outreach, a partnership, or public launch.

## Days 0-30: prove one cell can transact fairly

**Outcome:** 25 completed, undisputed jobs in two categories with a fully human-reviewed WhatsApp flow.

- Days 1-7: 25 worker and 25 hirer interviews; map trade shops, worker nodes, apartments, shops, support routes, local wage bands, and travel patterns.
- Days 8-14: onboard 30-40 workers, but count only those who pass a sample-offer response and comprehension check.
- Days 15-21: open to a private founding-hirer cohort. Human reviews every job card and match.
- Days 22-30: run one supply-channel test and one job-card copy test; review every cancellation, payment delay, safety report, and non-response.

**Scale to day 31 only if:** at least 60% of qualified jobs fill, 80% of agreed jobs complete, 90% of agreed wages are confirmed received, and support can meet its stated hours.

**Kill or redesign:** workers misunderstand the wage/scope card; jobs require repeated off-platform bargaining; nonpayment is common; serious safety cases cannot be handled; or the cell needs broad paid demand to produce its first jobs.

## Days 31-60: prove repeat behavior and channel quality

**Outcome:** 100 cumulative completed jobs and at least one acquisition source with repeatable cost per first completed job.

- Keep categories and geography fixed.
- Add a second worker node only if the first leaves a real supply gap.
- Test worker referral against a trusted trade-shop node.
- Test a hirer fee credit after completion against no referral incentive.
- Launch hire-again and saved-worker consent.
- Reduce human review only for low-risk, repeat pairs with unchanged scope patterns.
- Start cohort reporting by acquisition source.

**Scale:** 30%+ of eligible hirers repeat in their next need window, 40%+ of workers remain active at 30 days, p90 agreement time meets the published promise, and variable contribution has a believable path to positive.

**Kill a channel:** after 30 qualified leads, it produces fewer than 5 first completed jobs, creates high fraud/support load, or pulls workers from outside the serviceable cell.

## Days 61-90: prove replication, not just local heroics

**Outcome:** an adjoining cell reaches useful liquidity without borrowing most of the first cell's workers.

- Replicate the same two categories and materials.
- Train a second ambassador/operator from the written playbook.
- Hold product, fees, safety rules, and service hours constant.
- Compare time-to-liquidity, acquisition cost, support hours/job, wage receipt, and worker exposure.
- Add no new category until replication passes.

**Scale:** second cell reaches at least 80% of first-cell completion and payment guardrails within six weeks, with fewer than half its active workers borrowed from cell one.

**Stop replication:** economics require founder relationships that cannot be transferred, support quality drops, or the second cell's travel pattern destroys effective worker earnings.
