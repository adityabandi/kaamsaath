# V1 wedge decision: part-time home cooks in Bengaluru

## Locked v1 decision

The user selected **part-time recurring home cooks in Bengaluru** as v1. This is no longer an open cooks-versus-cleaners product choice. The field plan below validates the operating assumptions and determines whether to proceed, narrow, or stop, not which category to choose.

Start with **part-time recurring home cooks**, not a broad "maid" marketplace and not on-demand cleaning.

Use respectful, specific product categories: `home cook`, `household cleaner`, `laundry/utensils`, and later `broader domestic work`. Do not collapse workers into "maids" in product surfaces.

This is a strong hypothesis, not proven demand. Validate it in one East Bengaluru cell before changing the reusable platform architecture.

## Why cooks first

| Criterion | Part-time cooks | Household cleaners / broad domestic work |
|---|---:|---:|
| Repeat frequency / retention | 5 | 5 |
| Scope can be structured | 4 | 2 |
| Skill differentiation supports worker agency | 5 | 2 |
| Wage/fit variables visible upfront | 4 | 2 |
| Trial shift can be bounded | 4 | 3 |
| Substitution/replacement urgency | 3 | 4 |
| Home/privacy/safety burden | 2 | 1 |
| Scope-creep risk | 3 | 1 |
| Cold-start density | 4 | 5 |
| Product differentiation vs generic agencies | 4 | 2 |
| **Weighted recommendation** | **Lead** | **Later, separately designed** |

Scores are operator hypotheses for field validation, not market measurements.

Cooking has rich but legible fit: cuisine, dietary restrictions, meal count, people, prep/chopping/cleanup boundary, kitchen/equipment, groceries, timing, trial, holidays and monthly pay. That lets Jothe's scoping product create real value. Broad domestic cleaning carries especially high scope creep, caste/class/gender abuse, surveillance, key/property access, task bundling and "replacement guarantee" pressure. Do not launch cleaning by copying the cook flow.

## V1 job shape

Recurring, part-time home cooking in one 3-5 km cell:
- one or two visits/day, not live-in
- monthly relationship after one paid trial shift
- adult workers only
- normal home kitchens; no commercial catering, events, gas repair, deep cleaning or childcare
- worker chooses cuisines/tasks, areas, schedules, household types and minimum pay
- households specify people, meals/items, cuisine, veg/non-veg handling, allergies, timing, chopping/dishes boundary, groceries, equipment, kitchen access, pets, cameras, who is present, leave/holiday expectations, trial and monthly terms

## Cooks vs cleaners: falsifiable validation

Interview 30 cooks, 20 household cleaners, 30 hirers and 10 worker/trade/community nodes in the candidate cell. Pay workers for research time.

Run 20 fictional-card comprehension sessions per category and a manual availability map. Do not recruit or promise work.

Choose cooks only if:
- 60%+ of cook supply can describe a stable 30-minute travel cell and recurring availability
- 70%+ of household requests fit a shared scope schema without a bespoke negotiation
- median worker/hirer correction count is <=2 after first structured card
- likely monthly relationship value can support acquisition/support without worker commission
- workers prefer direct clear terms over agency-style placement
- safety/support cases appear operable inside stated staffing

Switch or redesign if cook schedules are too fragmented, replacement expectations require expensive standby supply, cuisine matching fragments liquidity, trials are commonly unpaid, or households insist on bundled cleaning/other work.

Do not select cleaners merely because demand is larger. Select only after a separate rights/safety design proves scope, pay, leave, keys/cameras, chemicals, harassment, workload and multi-household travel can be controlled.

## Cook Fair Job Card

- household size and adults/children, without unnecessary identity details
- exact meal/visit pattern and days
- cuisine and specific expected items
- dietary/allergy rules and veg/non-veg handling
- prep/chopping, serving, kitchen cleanup and utensils boundaries
- groceries/ingredients and menu planning responsibility
- kitchen equipment, gas/electric setup and known safety issues
- visit window and expected minutes
- trial date, duration and paid amount
- monthly wage, pay date, overtime/extra-person/guest/event rate
- travel allowance if any
- weekly day off, holidays, absences and notice
- cameras/pets/who is present/access/keys
- start date, review date and cancellation/notice terms
- direct UPI/cash preference

A household cannot add cleaning, childcare, errands, party cooking or extra homes without a versioned change and worker agreement.

## Matching

Hard filters: travel cell, schedule compatibility, cuisine/task capability, dietary handling preference, household/safety restrictions, work limits, blocks and pay floor.

Rank: schedule fit, travel cost, task match, worker preference, trusted repeat, reliability and exposure fairness. Do not rank on willingness to accept lower pay, fast replies alone, religion/caste, marital status, age beyond adult eligibility, or household discriminatory preference.

Reveal approximate locality, household size/meal scope, schedule, pay and access/safety facts before interest. Exact address and contact only after both confirm.

## Trial and recurring relationship

Trial shift is paid, scoped and not a disguised free audition. After it, both choose: start monthly agreement, another paid trial only for a specific justified gap, or no match. Declining after trial does not create a bad rating by itself.

Recurring card supports attendance exceptions, approved substitutes only with both parties' consent, leave, guest meals, extra cooking, pay changes and end notice. Jothe never promises an instant replacement; it can rematch within published, evidence-backed service levels.

## GTM change

Supply channels: cook networks, apartment staff networks, neighborhood grocery/vegetable shops, utensil/gas-service shops only as referral nodes, skilling/community groups and worker referrals. Demand: apartment clusters, resident connectors, working households and repeat referrals. Never let a building gatekeeper control worker access or wages.

Activation requires a schedule-compatible candidate pool, not generic registered cooks. Track liquidity by breakfast/lunch/dinner window, cuisine/task cluster and 15/30-minute travel band.

## Competition and legal/welfare context

Current products advertise Bengaluru cooks/domestic help, including BookMyBai, Broomees, EzyHelpers, Cookzy, MyHomeCook, Woostaa and Snabbit. Their sites establish active category competition, not service quality or market share.

Karnataka has current minimum-wage information and public debate/draft proposals around domestic-worker contracts, hours and welfare. Before launch, Karnataka labor counsel and domestic-worker organizations must review employment/intermediary classification, wage/leave/termination terms, welfare registration, harassment/safety, police/ID practices, consumer obligations and any enacted changes. Do not market a draft proposal as current law.
