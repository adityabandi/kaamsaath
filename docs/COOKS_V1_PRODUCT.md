# Jothe cooks v1

## Product

A WhatsApp-first way for Bengaluru households and part-time home cooks to create a clear paid trial, decide if the fit works, and maintain a fair recurring arrangement. Workers pay no commission and choose their cuisine, tasks, schedule, travel, household and pay preferences.

## Worker profile

- chosen name, adult status and assurance level
- languages and voice preference
- cuisines and dishes they are comfortable cooking
- vegetarian/non-vegetarian handling preferences
- allergy-process confidence, never medical certification unless actually verified
- household/meal capacity and visit duration
- prep/chopping/full-cooking/menu-planning/grocery/kitchen-cleanup boundaries
- breakfast/lunch/dinner windows by day
- service cell and maximum travel time
- minimum paid-trial and recurring wage expectations
- extra guest/meal/time rates
- weekly leave, holidays, notice and substitution preferences
- tools/transport; kitchen equipment requirements
- references, work samples or paid skill conversation with precise badge labels
- direct UPI/cash preference; UPI details protected until agreement
- camera/pet/occupied-home/access restrictions, trusted contact and support language

## Household request

Required before matching:
- approximate locality
- days and visit window
- adults/children count only as needed for meal volume
- meal and expected dishes/items
- cuisine and dietary handling
- allergies and cross-contamination expectations
- prep/chopping, cooking, menu/grocery, kitchen cleanup and utensils boundary
- ingredients/grocery responsibility
- equipment and known gas/electrical/kitchen issues
- paid-trial date, expected time and amount
- proposed monthly wage/pay date after trial or a stated decision process
- guest/extra-meal/overtime rates
- weekly off, holidays, absence and notice
- cameras, pets, who is present, entry/key/access and building requirements
- explicit exclusions: cleaning beyond agreed kitchen boundary, childcare, errands, events, other homes

Do not collect caste, religion, marital status or discriminatory preferences. Dietary/cuisine handling is represented as task capability or worker preference, not identity inference.

## Fair Cook Agreement

### Paid trial v1
Worker, household, location-after-match, date/window, people/meals, cuisine/diet, allergy instructions, exact task boundary, groceries, equipment, expected minutes, trial pay, extra rate, cancellation, access/safety, camera/pet disclosure, direct payment route and support.

### Recurring v2
Start date, days/windows, scope, monthly wage, pay date, weekly off, holidays, planned/unplanned absence, approved substitution, guests/extras, annual/periodic review, notice/end terms, key/access and direct UPI/cash. Any material change creates a new bilateral version.

A trial is paid. A second trial needs a clear reason and is paid. A worker declining the recurring arrangement after trial is not a negative rating event.

## Matching

Hard filters: schedule overlap, travel, cuisine/task capability, dietary-handling preferences, pay floor, household/access/safety restrictions, adult eligibility, consent, blocks and assurance requirement.

Ranking: schedule fit, expected travel, task fit, worker preference, repeat relationship, reliability and under-exposure correction. Never lower rank for declining work, taking leave, asking for a scope change, filing a safety/payment report, or refusing a discriminatory request.

## Lifecycle

`request -> scoped -> household_confirmed -> offered_small_batch -> worker_interested -> household_selected -> trial_agreed -> trial_arrived -> scope_change? -> trial_completed -> direct_upi_due -> dual_confirmed|disputed -> mutual_trial_review -> recurring_proposed -> recurring_agreed|closed`

Recurring: attendance/status, leave request, household cancellation, extras/change, payment due, confirmation, review and end notice.

No automatic replacement promise. Rematch is offered inside an honestly published service level. Substitutes are never sent into a home without both parties agreeing and receiving the same safety/scope information.

## Mutual reputation

Worker rates: scope accuracy, respectful behavior, camera/pet/access disclosure, safe kitchen, pay on time, leave/absence handling. Household rates: cooking/task representation, arrival communication, scope adherence and respectful behavior. Household food preference is not converted into a public "quality" accusation without structured context. Retaliatory ratings after pay/safety disputes are held.

## Safety

Home entry makes safety a primary gate:
- exact address after mutual agreement only
- who is present and camera/pet disclosure
- no hidden recording representations; counsel review needed
- key/access minimized; log handover/return if later supported
- worker check-in/trusted contact option
- gas smell, unsafe wiring/equipment, blocked exits, harassment or scope coercion -> leave/pause/human flow
- no pressure to handle allergens beyond agreed confidence/process
- household can report food-safety concern; product gives reviewed guidance and human support, not medical conclusions
- separate people after serious report; precaution is not guilt

## Direct UPI

Trial and recurring due amounts come from the current confirmed agreement/change. Household pays the worker directly. Worker UPI version/beneficiary is shown only after agreement; payer verifies the name in their app. Payer claim/screenshot/UTR is not settlement proof; worker confirms full/partial/not received. Jothe does not hold, split or refund money.

## Activation

Worker activated: completes cook profile, passes sample-card comprehension, and responds to a qualified schedule/cuisine offer. Household activated: confirms a complete paid-trial card. Marketplace activation: paid trial completed and worker confirms payment.

## Field validation

One East Bengaluru cell remains a hypothesis. Map supply by breakfast/lunch/dinner window, cuisine/task cluster and 15/30-minute travel. Interview cooks and households, test fictional cards and simulate matching before recruitment.

Proceed to a closed pilot only if schedule-compatible supply is dense enough, most demand fits the schema, trials are accepted as paid, support can handle home-entry risk, monthly relationship value can carry costs, and workers report the terms improve control. Stop or narrow on fragmented schedules, discriminatory demand, unpaid-trial pressure, bundled domestic work, intrusive verification, unsafe access or replacement expectations that require standby labor.
