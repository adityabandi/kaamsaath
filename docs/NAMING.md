# Working brand screen: Bengaluru home cooks v1

## Lead: Jothe

**Jothe** is the strongest working lead, not a legally cleared name. Kannada `ಜೊತೆ` points toward with/together, fitting a service built around mutually agreed household work rather than servant/employer language. It is short, Bengaluru-rooted and extensible beyond cooks.

Risks: Hindi/English speakers may mispronounce or not understand it; transliterations Jothe/Jote/Jothey may collide differently. Pair it with a plain descriptor in testing: **Jothe - home cooking work, clearly agreed.** Native Kannada/Hindi review is required.

Initial public search found no obvious exact-name Indian home-work marketplace. Search absence is weak evidence, not trademark/company/domain/app-store/social clearance.

## Shortlist

1. **Jothe** - locally rooted, warm, equal; pronunciation needs testing.
2. **GharSet** - clear household fit; feels transactional and may center the household.
3. **Sajha** - shared/together; common-word and Nepal associations weaken ownership.
4. **Saha** - short/supportive; broad surname/place use makes it hard to own.
5. **Namma Work** - Bengaluru belonging; generic and a public GST-name signal exists.

Rejected: Jothe (user finds it awkward/generic); Jothei, KaamSetu, HunarSetu, KaamMitra and Kaamly (visible active projects/products); Jodo (active Indian finance brand); AasPaas (active hyperlocal services); Oota/NammaCook (active food/company uses, category-bound); Cookzy, MyHomeCook, BookMyBai, Broomees and Snabbit (active category brands).

## Name field test

Test Jothe plus 2-3 alternatives with 15 cooks and 15 hirers across Kannada/Hindi/English:
- pronunciation/spelling after hearing once
- perceived service and whether it sounds like agency, charity or employer
- warmth, dignity and caste/class/gender/servitude associations
- WhatsApp-sender trust and one-day recall
- comfort saying "I got work through ___" and "I found a cook through ___"

Fail if >20% cannot recall/pronounce it, either side finds it demeaning, or meaning needs a long explanation.

Before public use: trademark/confusing-mark, MCA, domain, app-store, WhatsApp display-name, handle, native-language and counsel review. No purchase, filing, rename or launch is authorized.
