# Security/privacy/model incident runbook

- Declare owner, incident type, severity and time.
- Contain access or vendor/model route; never erase evidence.
- Protect affected people and maintain critical safety/opt-out paths.
- Start tamper-evident timeline and legal/privacy/security review.
- Determine scope, data classes, people, geography and ongoing risk.
- Check current CERT-In, DPDP, contract and other notification duties with counsel.
- Communicate facts, impact and protective actions without speculation.
- Restore in stages with monitoring.
- Complete root cause, control fixes, user remedy, vendor follow-up and council/transparency reporting where appropriate.
