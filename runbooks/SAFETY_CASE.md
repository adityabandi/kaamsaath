# Safety case runbook

1. Is the person in immediate danger? Use approved local-language leave/emergency guidance. Do not require evidence first.
2. Confirm safe channel/time to communicate.
3. Apply party separation and temporary feature restrictions proportionate to current risk.
4. Preserve the minimum platform-native record; ask for optional evidence only when safe.
5. Assign severity, owner and next-contact SLA. The model can suggest but cannot close severity.
6. Offer trusted-contact, medical/emergency and local support options from reviewed directory.
7. Investigate with separate interviews; avoid revealing the reporter.
8. Record finding standard, uncertainty, action, duration and reason.
9. Send appropriate decision and voice/text appeal route.
10. Review restoration and retaliation risk; close with retention/deletion date.
