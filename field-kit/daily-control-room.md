# Daily cell control room

## Start of day
- active worker coverage by category/time band
- jobs due today and agreement versions
- late/no-show risks
- open payment, dispute, safety and opt-out cases
- human/operator capacity

## During service hours
- qualified jobs awaiting first batch
- batches needing expansion
- worker exposure concentration
- messages outside service window needing an approved template
- low-confidence agent turns needing human handoff

## End of day
- amount due vs confirmed received
- cancellations/no-shows and rematch outcomes
- support response times
- safety and fraud cases
- tomorrow's supply gaps
- source cohort and variable cost updates
