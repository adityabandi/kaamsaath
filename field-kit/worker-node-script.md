# Worker node conversation - DRAFT, UNSENT

Use only after local-language review and approval.

## Opening
We are testing Jothe, a WhatsApp service for nearby repair work. Workers do not pay to join or apply, and Jothe does not deduct from the wage agreed for a job.

We are speaking with electricians and plumbers who work in [locality]. We want to understand how jobs arrive now, what makes a job unsafe or unprofitable, and whether a clear WhatsApp job card would help.

This is a pilot conversation, not a promise of work. May I explain it in two minutes?

## What to show
- one sample job card
- wage, extra-time rate, distance, tools and materials before acceptance
- worker can reply 1/2/3 or by voice
- hirers are rated for payment, safety and respect
- exact address appears only after both sides agree
- STOP pauses job alerts

## Never say
- guaranteed jobs or earnings
- fully verified or completely safe
- government approved
- instant payment protection
- that Aadhaar is required
- that referrals improve job ranking
