# Discovery interview guides - DRAFT, UNSENT

## Workers
1. Show me how the last three jobs reached you.
2. For each: travel, quoted wage, final wage, hours, materials, late payment, scope change.
3. What information would have made you decline before traveling?
4. Who do you trust for new work and why?
5. Play the sample voice job card. Ask the worker to explain it back, unaided.
6. What would make a WhatsApp job message feel fraudulent?
7. When and how often may job alerts arrive?
8. What must a hirer be rated on?

## Hirers
1. Reconstruct the last three local-worker hires.
2. Where did the referral come from? What failed?
3. Which terms were agreed before arrival?
4. What would you pay for faster, clearer matching while the worker keeps the full wage?
5. What proof helps without creating false security?
6. Would you use hire-again? Under what worker consent?
7. What support response is expected after no-show, damage, nonpayment allegation, or safety report?

Pay workers for research time. Do not treat research compensation as acquisition.
